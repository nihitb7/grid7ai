Objecto — AI-Powered OKR Platform

Objecto is an AI-powered platform built on OKR methodology to strengthen Alignment, Focus, Accountability, and Execution across your organization — cascading objectives from leadership down to every level, so each person can see exactly how their work drives company goals.

Today, many organizations struggle not because of a lack of talent, but because of fragmented execution, unclear priorities, and weak alignment. Objecto helps close that gap.

Track objective completion over time, with insights logged at each check-in.

See how objectives cascade and align across every level of the organization.

Alignment

Cascade objectives top-down and keep team goals aligned with organizational priorities.

Focus

Weekly reviews keep every person connected to their priorities and commitments.

Accountability

Clear ownership at every level, with progress and commitment visible across teams.

Objecto also applies time-series forecasting techniques (including ARIMA) to a team's own progress history — helping project whether an objective is on pace to hit its target, not just where it stands today. This capability is still maturing as we validate it against real customer data.

Case Study

Business Context

The organization was scaling rapidly, but leadership identified inconsistencies in execution across teams. Strategy communication was fragmented, employees were unclear on priorities, and multiple projects ran without measurable success indicators.

Business Challenges

Lack of alignment between leadership strategy and team execution, and confusion around priorities
Decisions made based on intuition instead of measurable goals
Delayed projects due to unclear ownership and accountability
No structured performance governance
Difficulty measuring impact and tracking progress

Solution Approach

AI-Enabled OKR Platform

Configured Grid Seven AI's tool Objecto to define and track OKRs, predict at-risk key results, identify bottlenecks, suggest priority realignment, and highlight low-performing areas.

Leadership Alignment Workshops

Conducted strategic workshops to translate high-level goals into measurable Objectives & Key Results.

Cross-Functional OKR Design

Created department-level and team-level OKRs aligned to company priorities.

Governance Cadence

Established weekly and monthly review cycles with transparent dashboards.

Outcomes & Impact

Clear Organizational Focus

Everyone understands top priorities and what truly matters.

Better Goal Alignment

Teams align work with company objectives, reducing silos.

Improved Transparency

Goals and progress are visible across the organization.

Higher Employee Engagement

People feel connected to meaningful goals.

Faster Decision-Making

Priorities are clear, reducing confusion and delays.

Data-Driven Performance Tracking

Progress measured objectively through key results.

Increased Productivity

Teams focus on high-impact work instead of routine tasks.

Enhanced Performance Culture

Focus has shifted from effort to outcomes.

Reduced Misalignment

Redundant and conflicting work has been reduced.

Continuous Improvement

Regular reviews foster learning and iteration.
